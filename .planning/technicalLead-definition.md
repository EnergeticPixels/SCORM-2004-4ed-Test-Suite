## Technical Lead (TL): General Definition

A Technical Lead (TL) is typically the role accountable for technical direction and technical decision quality for a team.

### Core Responsibilities
1. Set the technical approach for planned work.
1. Break complex initiatives into implementable engineering tasks.
1. Make or facilitate key technical decisions when tradeoffs arise.
1. Review architecture and code for correctness, maintainability, and risk.
1. Unblock engineers on difficult implementation problems.
1. Align technical choices with product goals, timelines, and constraints.
1. Maintain technical quality standards (testing, reliability, security, architecture discipline).

### What a TL Usually Is Not
1. Not only a people manager (a TL may have no direct reports).
1. Not just the strongest coder working alone.
1. Not a project manager, though TLs work closely with PMs.

### Common Role Boundaries
1. PM generally owns what is built and by when.
1. TL generally owns how it is built and whether the approach is technically sound.
1. Engineering Manager generally owns team health, capacity, and staffing (varies by organization).

### Practical TL Outcomes
1. Clear technical direction for the team.
1. Better decision consistency across contributors.
1. Faster issue resolution when blockers appear.
1. Lower long-term maintenance risk through sound engineering choices.

### Alternative Titles
- Engineering Lead
- Technical Owner
- Architecture Lead
